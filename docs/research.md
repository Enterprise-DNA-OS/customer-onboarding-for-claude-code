Rocketlane: US$20,700/year is a modelled 25-person Premium subscription at US$69 per person per month billed annually. It is not a verified customer invoice, a claim of local usage or an Enterprise DNA price. Implementation is separately scoped by the vendor. No local evidence verified a customer currently paying Rocketlane, so buyer fit is scored conservatively. The partner-research snapshot names its partner programme but is not proof of a customer relationship.
